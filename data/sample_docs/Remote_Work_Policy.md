# Johns Hopkins University Remote Work Policy
## Effective Date: January 2025

### Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for remote and hybrid work arrangements to support workforce flexibility while maintaining operational effectiveness and institutional excellence.

### Scope
This policy applies to all benefits-eligible staff and faculty positions, excluding:
- Positions requiring physical presence (labs, clinical care, facilities)
- Temporary or contract workers
- Student employees

### Work Arrangements

#### Fully Remote
- Work performed exclusively from approved remote location
- Must reside within approved states (MD, DC, VA, PA, NY, DE)
- Requires department head and HR approval
- Subject to annual review

#### Hybrid
- Combination of on-campus and remote work
- Minimum 2 days on-campus per week (unless approved otherwise)
- Core hours: Tuesday-Thursday encouraged for in-person collaboration
- Flexible schedule with supervisor approval

#### In-Office
- Traditional on-campus work arrangement
- Standard expectation unless other arrangements approved

### Eligibility Requirements

Employees must demonstrate:
1. **Performance**: Meets or exceeds expectations for past 12 months
2. **Role Suitability**: Job duties can be performed remotely without diminishing quality
3. **Technology Proficiency**: Competent with collaboration tools and remote work technology
4. **Communication**: Proven ability to work independently and communicate effectively
5. **Tenure**: Minimum 6 months in current role (waived for new hires in approved remote positions)

### Application Process

1. Employee submits Remote Work Agreement form via Workday
2. Supervisor reviews and provides recommendation
3. Department head approves or denies request
4. HR reviews for policy compliance
5. IT provisions necessary equipment and access
6. Agreement executed for specified duration (typically 6-12 months)

### Equipment and Technology

**University Provided:**
- Laptop computer (refresh every 4 years)
- Monitor (for hybrid/remote workers)
- Docking station or adapters
- Headset for video conferencing
- VPN access and necessary software licenses

**Employee Responsible For:**
- High-speed internet (minimum 25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload)
- Dedicated workspace
- Ergonomic furniture
- Home office supplies beyond computer equipment

**Stipends:**
- Remote workers: $50/month internet reimbursement
- Hybrid workers: $25/month internet reimbursement
- One-time home office setup: Up to $500 (receipts required)

### Work Expectations

**Availability:**
- Available during core business hours (9 AM - 3 PM local time)
- Responsive to emails within 4 hours during business hours
- Attend all required meetings (virtual or in-person)
- Calendar must reflect availability and working location

**Communication:**
- Daily check-ins with team via Slack or Teams
- Weekly one-on-one with supervisor
- Video on for meetings (camera required for remote workers)
- Update Outlook calendar with work location daily

**Productivity:**
- Maintain same performance standards as in-office work
- Meet all deadlines and deliverables
- Participate actively in team collaboration
- Document work progress in project management tools

**Security:**
- Use only JHU-issued devices for university work
- Enable full disk encryption
- Use VPN for all university system access
- Lock screen when stepping away
- No sharing of workspace with non-JHU individuals during work hours
- Shred or securely dispose of confidential documents
- Report security incidents immediately

### Performance Management

Remote work agreements may be modified or terminated if:
- Performance declines below acceptable standards
- Communication or availability issues arise
- Business needs change
- Security or compliance violations occur
- Equipment or technology issues cannot be resolved

Supervisors will conduct quarterly reviews of remote work arrangements.

### Travel and Expenses

**On-Campus Requirements:**
When remote workers are required to come to campus:
- Advance notice: Minimum 2 weeks for planned visits
- Travel expenses: Not reimbursable for local travel (within 50 miles)
- Parking: Temporary permits available through supervisor
- Workspace: Hoteling desks available in most buildings

**Out-of-State Remote Workers:**
- Required campus visits: Minimum quarterly (or as role requires)
- Travel expenses: Reimbursable per university travel policy
- Accommodation: Hotel costs covered for overnight stays

### Tax and Legal Considerations

**Tax Implications:**
- Remote work location may affect state tax withholding
- Employees responsible for understanding tax obligations
- Consult with tax advisor for multi-state implications

**Workers' Compensation:**
- Coverage extends to approved remote workspace
- Must report work-related injuries immediately
- Home office safety standards must be maintained

**Compliance:**
- Remote work must comply with local zoning and HOA regulations
- International remote work prohibited without prior legal review
- Immigration status may affect remote work eligibility

### Termination of Remote Work

Arrangements may be terminated with:
- 30 days notice for performance or business reasons
- Immediate termination for security violations or policy breaches
- Mutual agreement between employee and supervisor

Upon termination of remote work:
- Employee returns to on-campus work arrangement
- University equipment must be returned within 5 business days
- No guarantee of specific workspace or location on campus

### Dispute Resolution

Concerns or disputes should be escalated as follows:
1. Direct supervisor
2. Department head
3. HR Business Partner
4. Office of Institutional Equity (if discrimination alleged)

### Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed annually and updated as needed. Employees with active remote work agreements will be notified of changes and given 60 days to adapt to new requirements.
