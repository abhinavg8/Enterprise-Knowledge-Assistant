JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY
EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE
Academic Year 2025-2026

HEALTH INSURANCE

Medical Plans:
Johns Hopkins offers three medical plan options through CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield:

1. Traditional Plan (PPO)
   - Employee Only: $180/month
   - Employee + Spouse: $420/month
   - Employee + Children: $380/month
   - Family: $580/month
   - Deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family
   - Out-of-pocket max: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family
   - Coverage: 80/20 after deductible

2. High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA
   - Employee Only: $85/month
   - Employee + Spouse: $240/month
   - Employee + Children: $210/month
   - Family: $360/month
   - Deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
   - Out-of-pocket max: $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
   - HSA Employer Contribution: $750 individual / $1,500 family

3. HMO Plan
   - Employee Only: $120/month
   - Employee + Spouse: $320/month
   - Employee + Children: $290/month
   - Family: $480/month
   - No deductible
   - $25 copay primary care / $40 specialist
   - Requires PCP selection and referrals

Dental Insurance:
Two options available through Delta Dental:
- Basic Plan: $25/month employee only, covers preventive 100%, basic 80%, major 50%
- Premium Plan: $45/month employee only, covers preventive 100%, basic 90%, major 70%
- Annual maximum: $2,000 Basic / $3,000 Premium

Vision Insurance:
Through VSP Vision Care:
- Cost: $8/month employee only
- Annual eye exam covered 100%
- Frames allowance: $150 every calendar year
- Lens coverage: Standard single vision, lined bifocal, or trifocal
- Contact lenses: $150 allowance in lieu of glasses

RETIREMENT PLANS

403(b) Retirement Plan:
- Eligibility: Immediate for all employees
- Employee contribution: Up to IRS maximum ($23,000 for 2025)
- Employer match: 100% of first 5% of salary contributed
- Vesting: Immediate for both employee and employer contributions
- Catch-up contributions: $7,500 for age 50+ (2025 limit)

Pension Plan (for eligible faculty):
- Eligibility: Full-time faculty with 5+ years of service
- Benefit: 1.5% of average final compensation × years of service
- Vesting: 5 years of service
- Retirement age: Normal retirement at 65, early at 55 with reduced benefits

PAID TIME OFF

Vacation:
- 0-5 years of service: 15 days/year
- 5-10 years: 20 days/year
- 10+ years: 25 days/year
- Maximum accrual: 300 hours
- Unused vacation may be carried over up to maximum

Sick Leave:
- Accrual: 1 day per month (12 days/year)
- No maximum accrual
- Can be used for personal illness, family care, or medical appointments

Holidays:
13 paid holidays including:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving (2 days)
- Winter Break (December 24-January 1)

Personal Days:
3 personal days per calendar year, non-cumulative

LEAVE PROGRAMS

Parental Leave:
- 8 weeks paid leave for birth, adoption, or foster placement
- Available to all benefits-eligible employees
- Must be taken within 12 months of event

Family Medical Leave (FMLA):
- Up to 12 weeks unpaid leave for qualifying events
- Job protection during leave
- Benefits continuation during leave

Short-Term Disability:
- Waiting period: 7 days
- Benefit: 60% of salary up to $2,000/week
- Duration: Up to 26 weeks
- University-paid benefit

Long-Term Disability:
- Waiting period: 180 days
- Benefit: 60% of salary up to $10,000/month
- Duration: To age 65 or recovery
- University-paid benefit

EDUCATION BENEFITS

Tuition Remission:
- Full-time employees: 100% tuition for undergraduate or graduate courses at JHU
- Part-time employees (20+ hours/week): 50% tuition
- Dependent children: 50% undergraduate tuition at JHU or $5,250/year at other accredited institutions
- Eligibility: After 1 year of continuous service

Professional Development:
- $2,500 annual allowance for conferences, certifications, and training
- Additional funds available through departmental budgets
- Required pre-approval from supervisor

WELLNESS PROGRAMS

Fitness Center Membership:
- Free access to Recreation Center and Fitness facilities
- Group fitness classes included
- Personal training available at discounted rates

Employee Assistance Program (EAP):
- Confidential counseling services
- 8 free sessions per issue per year
- 24/7 crisis support hotline: 1-800-JHU-WELL
- Work-life resources and referrals

Wellness Incentive Program:
- Earn up to $300 annual credit toward health insurance premiums
- Activities: Annual physical, preventive screenings, fitness challenges
- Track progress through HealthyHop portal

COMMUTER BENEFITS

Transit Subsidy:
- Up to $150/month pre-tax for public transportation
- Eligible costs: Metro, bus, commuter rail

Parking:
- On-campus parking available at subsidized rates
- Rates vary by location ($80-$150/month)
- Hybrid workers: Part-time parking options available

Bike Share:
- 50% discount on annual membership
- Bike storage and shower facilities available

ENROLLMENT AND CHANGES

Open Enrollment:
Annual enrollment period: November 1-30 for January 1 effective date

Qualifying Life Events:
Changes outside open enrollment allowed within 31 days of:
- Marriage or divorce
- Birth or adoption
- Loss of other coverage
- Change in employment status
